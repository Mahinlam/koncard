About KONCARD

KONCARD (কোন কার্ড?) is a Bangladesh-focused credit-card discovery project built around one simple question: Which card is right for you?

What we do

We turn a complicated set of card features into a short questionnaire and a simple recommendation.

What we don't do

We do not issue cards, approve applications, access bank accounts, ask for NID numbers or guarantee approval.
