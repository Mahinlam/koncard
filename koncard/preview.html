      KONCARD

      Methodology

      KONCARD's first version is a preference-matching tool. It does not claim to predict approval.

      How the score works

      We map your answers to editorial preference weights, then score each card in the current dataset. The score is a product recommendation indicator, not an official bank score.

      Why there's no income question

      An earlier build asked about monthly income but never used the answer, and no card had income data to match against. That question was removed rather than left in as decoration. It returns once income eligibility is confirmed per card and the scoring logic actually uses it.

      Verification

      Each launch card has a verification flag and date. When a fee, eligibility rule or promotion has not been checked, KONCARD leaves it blank rather than guess.
